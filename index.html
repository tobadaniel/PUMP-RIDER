How to ride

  1. One button. Hold to climb, release to dive. Feather it — momentum is everything.
  2. Orbs pay 20 points times your combo multiplier. Chain them without missing to reach x4. One miss and the combo is gone.
  3. Close calls pay +15: pass within a whisker of the chart without touching it. Risky, repeatable, addictive.
  4. Rug pulls are telegraphed: warning first, then the cliff, then the dead-cat bounce. The bounce is the dangerous part — it comes back up at you.
  5. The chart scrolls faster the longer you live. Your score is distance + orbs + bonuses.
